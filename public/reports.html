Report 1: Members Plan & Trainer Allocations

Fetches every member alongside their corresponding membership plans and assigned personal trainers.

Show SQL Query
SELECT m.id, m.name, m.email, mb.plan_type, mb.price, t.name as trainer_name FROM members m LEFT JOIN memberships mb ON m.membership_id = mb.id LEFT JOIN trainers t ON m.trainer_id = t.id ORDER BY m.id ASC;

Report 2: Total Attendance Visits per Member

Aggregates check-in records to count total visits for each individual gym member.

Show SQL Query
SELECT m.name, COUNT(a.id) as total_visits FROM members m LEFT JOIN attendance a ON m.id = a.member_id GROUP BY m.id ORDER BY total_visits DESC;

Report 3: Financial Revenue Breakdown by Membership Plan

Correlates plans with member enrollments and sums up payment histories to show revenue by membership type.

Show SQL Query
SELECT mb.plan_type, COUNT(DISTINCT m.id) as member_count, COALESCE(SUM(p.amount), 0) as total_revenue FROM memberships mb LEFT JOIN members m ON m.membership_id = mb.id LEFT JOIN payments p ON p.member_id = m.id GROUP BY mb.id ORDER BY total_revenue DESC;
